Keywords

political science, Latin America, legislative oversight, PhD candidate, democracy

Teaching

I have taught undergraduate and graduate courses in political science, public policy, and research methodology at various academic institutions in Latin America. My teaching focuses on the intersection of institutions, behavior, and methods, combining theoretical foundations with applied, data-driven approaches.
